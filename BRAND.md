# Moonshot: brand notes (hypothetical)

**Moonshot** is a sparkling energy drink from Pune for people who work late. 80 mg of caffeine from
green coffee and green tea, real fruit, electrolytes, and 5 g of sugar a can.

## Palette roles (same roles as the reference site, Moonshot's own colours)

Dark and moonlit. Night page `#0D0B21`, moonlight text `#F3EEE0`, raised panels (cards, drawers, menus)
`#1A1640`, gold `#FFC75A` (default button, Add to cart, bandeau, cart badge) with a moonlight drop shadow.
Each flavour paints the hero, its can and its tiles in a deep night colour and lights them with a bright accent.
The hero, intro and footer carry a faint starfield; the can floats in a soft moon halo, and the footer game
runs under a crescent moon.

## Type and art

- Inter Tight, light and all caps for the big lines.
- A chunky custom MOONSHOT wordmark, set vertically on the can.
- Hand-built flat illustrations: a dancing mango, a kala-khatta gola in sunglasses, a guava-chilli pair,
  a nimbu-mirchi charm, and Chhotu the footer-game mascot.

## Voice

Playful and honest. "We don't deliver to the moon yet. ISRO, call us."
